The thesis

You don’t have to do everything anymore. You need to learn how to direct the people who do.Prompt → context → agent → responsibility → team of agents → agentic organization

What you’ll know how to do

One agent, six deliverables

1

Autonomy map — ten activities from your work rated from 0 to 5, and the one that becomes your agent.

2

Persistent agent — a profile in five sections, running in an AI chat, that stops on Friday and resumes on Monday.

3

Delegation map — what stays with people, what goes to AI, what becomes an agent's responsibility, with criteria and a metric.

4

Autonomy matrix — what it does alone, what requires approval, what it never does, and the shutoff button.

5

Trust scorecard — five numbers a week instead of "did it work?", and the decision to scale up or down.

6

Agentic company — the manager's manual and the team design: director, specialists, evaluator, and you making the final decision.

Who it’s for

Made for managers, not programmers

is for you if you

  • You’re an HR manager, marketing coordinator, customer support supervisor, store owner, or department lead, and you already use chat AI for one-off tasks.
  • You want AI to take on an ongoing responsibility in your area, with oversight, not just “help with writing.”
  • You’ve never programmed, don’t use the command line, and prefer to learn by doing, at your own pace.

it’s not for you if

  • You want to build agents with code: this course is about delegating, controlling, and managing, not programming.
  • You’re looking for a course with traditional video lessons. This course uses active reading, with practice and review built in.
